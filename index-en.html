Certification

We hold the official ISO 9001:2015 certification, which not only ensures the quality of our processes but also reflects our commitment to:

  • Excellence.
  • Continuous improvement.
  • Operational efficiency.
  • Customer satisfaction.

CQS-UK is a British organization specialized in management system certification, focused on ISO standards such as ISO 9001. Its main mission is to help companies, both nationally and internationally, demonstrate:

  • their commitment to superior quality standards,
  • permanent innovation in processes and services,
  • resource optimization for solid and effective performance, and
  • the building of trust-based and value-driven relationships with their clients.

By obtaining this certification, Metalmecánica TAY demonstrates and reaffirms its commitment to quality and continuous improvement, strengthening its presence as a solid partner in the market and ensuring peace of mind for its clients in every project delivered.

This recognition supports our commitment to precision and excellence, reflecting the constant dedication of our team to:

  • Maintain superior quality standards.
  • Drive continuous improvement.
  • Guarantee reliable solutions that inspire confidence in every project.

The scope of our certification is as follows:
"Manufacturing and Repair of hydraulic, pneumatic, and mechanical components and systems, including associated workshop work."
